# Keeva Brand Guidelines

## 1. Brand Identity
**Keeva** is a private, premium media keeper for temporary moments.
Emotional premise: *"Keep the moments that matter."*

## 2. Master Brand Palette
- **Obsidian Dark (Canvas):** `#090B0E` (RGB: 9, 11, 14)
- **Obsidian Surface:** `#161A22` (RGB: 22, 26, 34)
- **Aurora Mint (Primary):** `#10B981` (RGB: 16, 185, 129)
- **Aurora Indigo (Accent):** `#6366F1` (RGB: 99, 102, 241)
- **Crisp Off-White (Text):** `#F9FAFB` (RGB: 249, 250, 251)
- **Muted Slate (Secondary):** `#9CA3AF` (RGB: 156, 163, 175)

## 3. Clear Space & Scaling
- The logo mark must maintain a minimum clear margin of `0.5H` on all four sides.
- Minimum digital display width for the mark: `24dp`.
- Minimum digital display width for the combined lockup: `80dp`.

## 4. Typography
- Primary Display & Body: **Plus Jakarta Sans** (SemiBold for headings, Regular for body).
- Numerical / Duration / Metrics: **Plus Jakarta Sans** with tabular figures (`tnum`).

## 5. Misuse Prohibitions
- Never rotate or warp the logo mark.
- Never use WhatsApp green (`#25D366`) or display phone bubble icons.
- Never place the dark logo variant on mid-tone gray backgrounds without proper contrast.
- Never use the word "Download" when referring to keeping moments in Keeva.

## 6. Brand Name Status & Trademark
- **Development Name:** "Keeva" is approved as the internal development and testing brand name for this project.
- **Trademark Notice:** Formal trademark search, clearance, and registration across target commercial jurisdictions remains an explicit pre-release legal milestone prior to public distribution.
